## Look

Robert played with brightness at 3.00x, the slider maximum for an 800-nit peak (F1 readout: white 800 nits, 18 % gray 70 nits), and judged the image good ("normalne funguje"). Peak override stayed at auto.

## HDR meter

Second run with `SHADPS4_HDR_METER=1` (set in the launching shell; `setx` alone does not reach an already open PowerShell), brightness 3.00x:

| Scene | peak | 99.9 % | 99 % | median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Dream balcony, sun in view | 799 | 799 | 761 | 5 |
| Yharnam street, lamp and messengers | 799 | 420 | 181 | 10 |
| Yharnam bridge, overcast sky | 799 | 200 | 116 | 3 |

Clipped SDR white lands at the configured 800 nits, so the curve, the scRGB scaling and the brightness clamp agree on screen with the CPU mirror. Medians stay at 3 to 10 nits: shadows keep the dark SDR look, the boost goes into highlights.

## Verdict

RetroHDR runs as designed on the target host: scRGB session starts, the Perceptual Boost output looks right at the chosen brightness, the overlay and screenshots work, and frame rate is unaffected. The HDR meter confirms the output level and the live toggle works. Items 3, 6 and 7 were skipped.
